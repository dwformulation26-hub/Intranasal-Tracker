# Intranasal Pipeline Radar — weekly update, 2026-10-22

**Headline:** Three new intranasal products added (NeOnc's NEO100 for glioma, Hikma's investigational epinephrine
nasal spray, and LTR Pharma's SPONTAN/ROXUS vardenafil spray for ED); Firebrick's Nasodine picked up Philippines
approval and Dompé's nasal NGF (cenegermin) turned out to also be in Phase 2 for cerebral palsy, alongside its
known NAION Phase 3. 124 cached leads and 1 trial lead triaged; 25 cards rotation-checked; 7 news items added.

## New products added
- **neonc-neo100** — NEO100 (NeOnc Technologies), intranasal perillyl alcohol for recurrent/progressive high-grade
  glioma. UAE Department of Health-Abu Dhabi granted an IND (Jun 2026); US NEO100-01 Phase 2a fully enrolled.
  Source: NeOnc GlobeNewswire release via SEC 8-K (23 Jun 2026).
- **hikma-epinephrine-nasal** — Hikma's investigational epinephrine nasal spray for anaphylaxis. FDA accepted a
  505(b)(2) NDA (Sep 2026); EU/UK hybrid dossier accepted via DCP/National Procedure; Canada filed.
  Source: Hikma press release (9 Sep 2026).
- **ltr-spontan-roxus** — SPONTAN / ROXUS (LTR Pharma), intranasal vardenafil for erectile dysfunction. SPONTAN is
  supplied in Australia under the TGA Special Access Scheme; ROXUS, the US compounded version, had its first US
  prescriptions filled (Oct 2026) via Shed's Mavrox telehealth platform and Strive Pharmacy (Section 503A).
  Source: LTR Pharma ASX announcement (6 Oct 2026).

## Card updates
- **nasodine** (Firebrick Pharma): Philippines FDA approved Nasodine as an OTC antiseptic nasal spray (valid to
  Sep 2028) — its 5th approved market after the US, Singapore, Fiji and Indonesia. Source: Firebrick ASX
  announcement (16 Oct 2026).
- **cenegermin-naion** (Dompé): added a second, separate indication — a Phase 2 trial (NGF-CP-201, n=60) of
  intranasal cenegermin in children aged 2–6 with spastic cerebral palsy, recruiting in Italy since Jan 2026 —
  alongside the already-tracked NAION Phase 3 (Galassia-NAION-301). Source: EU CTIS trial registry (2025-522786-29-00).
- **valtoco** (Neurelis): added a completed pediatric PK study (NCT05076838, n=36) to the card's trial list, found
  via this week's trial-discovery lead; cleared the `noTrial` placeholder text since the trial is now linked.
