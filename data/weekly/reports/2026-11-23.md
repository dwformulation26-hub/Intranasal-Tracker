# Weekly update — 2026-11-16 to 2026-11-23

**Headline:** A quiet week for new products, but five active cards got material updates: NeOnc's NEO100 Phase 2a objective response rose to 12.5% from 8.3%, Nasus Pharma's NS002 beat an autoinjector on epinephrine speed under nasal congestion at ACAAI 2026, ALK's EURneffy won an 88% public-place preference over auto-injectors in a Canadian study, and Scienture's REZENOPY delivered its first full quarter of revenue (+265%). A rotation check also turned up two "found late" corrections: Sprix changed hands from Assertio to Cosette Pharmaceuticals back in April, and LTR Pharma's SPONTAN posted final Phase II data in October that hadn't yet reached the tracker.
